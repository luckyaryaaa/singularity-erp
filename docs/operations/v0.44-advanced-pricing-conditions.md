# v0.44 — Advanced Pricing Conditions Stage 1

Migration 079 menambahkan condition records server-authoritative untuk base
price, diskon nominal/persen, dan surcharge. Resolver mempertimbangkan legal
entity, produk, pelanggan, kategori, minimum quantity, validity, priority, dan
status aktif. Perubahan menggunakan optimistic lock, idempotency, scope check,
dan audit.

Stage berikutnya tetap mencakup rebate, commission settlement, serta auto-fill
harga ke line quotation/Sales Order dengan snapshot pricing provenance.
